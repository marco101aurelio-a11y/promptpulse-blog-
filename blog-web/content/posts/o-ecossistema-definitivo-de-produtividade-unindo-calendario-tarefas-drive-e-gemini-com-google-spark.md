## **How It Works: A Step-by-Step Example**  

Let’s break down how this system functions in real life:  

1. **Capture Ideas**: You jot down a task in Google Keep.  
2. **Auto-Processing**: Gemini reads the note and creates a task in Google Tasks.  
3. **Smart Scheduling**: Gemini assigns a time block and creates the task in Google Tasks in Google Calendar based on priority.  
4. **Automatic Documentation**: Once complete, Gemini saves the result in Google Drive, filing it in the right folder.  

This is just one example. With Gemini and Google Tasks, you can build custom automations for almost anything—weekly reports, project management, and more.
