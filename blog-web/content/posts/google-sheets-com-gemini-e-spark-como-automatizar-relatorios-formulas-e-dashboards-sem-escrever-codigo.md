## **2. 3 Practical Examples: Automating Sheets with Tasks and Gemini**  

### **📊 Example 1: Automatically Updating Sales Reports**  
**Problem:** Every week, you extract data from your CRM, filter sales by region, and create a chart.  
**Solution with Gemini + Tasks:**  
1. **Connect Google Forms** (where your team logs sales) to Sheets.  
2. **Ask Gemini:**  
   > *"Create a dashboard showing total sales, top performer, and regional breakdown. Update automatically when new data arrives."*  
3. Automate via **Google Tasks & Gemini**:  
   - **Trigger**: New entry in Forms → **Action**: Update dashboard  

### **📆 Example 2: Automatically Scheduling Calendar Events from Sheets**  
Imagine a **spreadsheet of appointments** that automatically schedules them in **Google Calendar**:  
1. **In Sheets**, ask Gemini:  
   > *"Create an automation that adds events to Google Calendar whenever a date is entered in the 'Meeting' column."*  
2. **Google Tasks & Gemini** handle the workflow:  
   - **If** new row added → **Then** create event in Calendar  

> 💡 **Pro Tip:** This same logic applies to **tasks**, as we explained in **[From Keep to Final Report: Automated Integration](/posts/do-keep-ao-relatorio-final-como-integrar-tarefas-drive-docs-e-spark-no-piloto-automatico)**.  

### **📉 Example 3: Code-Free Data Analysis with Gemini**  
**Let’s say you want to:**  
> *"Extract the top 5 customers by sales and send them a personalized thank-you email."*  

**How to do it:**  
1. **Gemini in Sheets:**  
   - Identifies top customers with **AI-powered sorting**.  
2. **Gemini + Gmail & Tasks:**  
   - **Trigger**: Spreadsheet updated → **Action**: Send personalized email
